Modal with an iframe:

clicking a video card opens a modal with an iframe. 
Google Drive videos can be embedded using the /preview endpoint instead of /view. 
For example:
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rzyexHmVS89tR3tnr4bwO_vzqBSDjNlb/view?usp=sharing
Embed: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rzyexHmVS89tR3tnr4bwO_vzqBSDjNlb/preview


Firefox's responsive design mode (Ctrl+Shift+M) and toggle mobile emulation to verify the white page is gone.
